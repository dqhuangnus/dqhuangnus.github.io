I am a PhD student at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/), based in the [CRISP group](https://lorejam.wixsite.com/crisp) at the UCL East campus, supervised by [Dr. Lorenzo Jamone](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/102308-lorenzo-jamone). I started my PhD in February 2026. My research focuses on tactile sensing for contact-rich robot manipulation, with a particular interest in demonstrating where direct-force tactile sensing (magnetic transduction, true 3-axis force vectors, high bandwidth) provides value that vision-based sensing and learning-only approaches cannot match.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in Robotics at the National University of Singapore and a BEng in Automation at Beihang University.

## Research

My current work is organized around three connected questions:

- **Tactile as contact state observation** — using tactile feedback to detect and recover from failures during reactive manipulation (CRAFT, under review at IEEE RA-L).
- **Tactile as physical property inference** — inferring physical properties such as mass and stiffness from tactile signals to generalize manipulation policies across visually identical but physically different objects.
- **Tactile-driven action selection** — using an online belief over inferred tactile properties to drive discrete action selection ("feel-then-commit") with no human demonstrations required.

See the [Publications](/publications/) page for a full list of my papers.
