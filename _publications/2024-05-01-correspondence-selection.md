Establishing robust correspondences between images is central to many computer vision tasks, but incorrect matches are common due to illumination, occlusion, and other factors. This paper proposes SOSPC, an algorithm that removes incorrect correspondences using two constraints: an object-matching step (built on GMS-selected correspondences) to keep matches on the same object, and an iterative, clustering-based fundamental matrix estimation to keep matches at the same relative position. The method achieves state-of-the-art performance across five datasets.
