This study presents bulk atmospheric microplastic (MP) deposition data collected over individual rainfall events across a 12-month period (Aug 2020 &ndash; Aug 2021) in a rural region of Quzhou County, North China Plain. MPs were quantified using fluorescence microscopy and characterized chemically via micro-FTIR. The measured atmospheric MP deposition rate was one to two orders of magnitude higher than comparable studies, with a positive correlation observed between MP deposition and rainfall; sources were traced using a HYSPLIT back-trajectory model.
